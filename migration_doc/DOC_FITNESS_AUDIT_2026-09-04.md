# Documentation Fitness Audit — 2026-09-04

**Subject:** the CesiumJS-WebGPU fork's documentation set, judged for fitness for purpose, not prose quality.

**Method:** five independent lenses (DEVELOPMENT, PLANNING, SCOPING, WELL-BUILT, STRUCTURE & HYGIENE), each finding refuted by a separate agent that re-read the cited lines and the code at HEAD. Only CONFIRMED and CORRECTED findings survive into §3; REFUTED findings are listed at the end of §3.

## 1. The answer

**Do we have good architecture docs that are useful for development, planning, and keeping features well scoped and well built?**

Partly — the corpus contains several of the best individual documents I have seen in a fork this size, and it fails as a *system* on the two axes that decide fitness: routing and currency. **For a developer (2/5)** the answer is no: the one genuinely excellent developer doc, `FEATURE_RENDERER_ONBOARDING.md`, is invisible from every entry point (`CLAUDE.md`, `.clinerules` and `AGENTS.md` name it zero times, and `README.md`'s START-HERE table at `:133-154` is 18 governance rows), four core topics have no narrative anywhere but source docstrings (frame flow, uniform-buffer packing, the cache map, the picking producer contract), and the two procedures a renderer-facing change most often needs are unfollowable at HEAD — `CLAUDE.md:226-232`'s "add a define bit" assumes free bits in a word that is full (`WebGPUShaderDefines.ts:863`, verified: 31 entries over bits 0-30), and `FEATURE_RENDERER_ONBOARDING.md:46/:175-188` inverts the lazy-loader contract against `GraphicsContext.ts:2050-2053` in exactly the direction the generation guard exists to prevent. **For a lead (3/5)** the orientation layer is genuinely strong — `CAMPAIGN_STATE.md` gives the whole portfolio in one 350-line read, and the newest queues carry the best-designed row cards in the repo — but the row layer beneath it is not maintained: planning the next five rows from the docs alone put two already-landed rows into a wave (`AR-002` landed Batch 1415, `AR-751` Batch 1416, both still open at `QUEUE_2026-09-03_ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW.md:103/:135`), found a third open in the ledger and landed in the dispatch view, and could not locate a fourth in its own campaign's authority; and every sizing number a lead would quote is wrong by 40-380%. **For a reviewer/scoper (3/5)** the ceiling is high and the floor is where a newcomer lands: where the corpus is good it is exceptional (`DEFERRED_WORK.md`'s `UP144-VECTOR-LAYER-WGSL` entry is a complete, liftable scope with the rejected design, the binding budget, the mutation set and the owed gate), but three pointers a newcomer is told to trust are false at HEAD, the rules that define "complete" — Principles 5/6/9/10, the proof bar, the wave-end gate — live in a gitignored file whose tracked substitute predates all of them by five months, and the instrument Principle 6 makes mandatory carries no coupling data at all. The **well-built** dimension is weakest (2/5): the runner-home rule is violated by 235 of 331 specs while its census runs non-strict and in no CI job, eleven of fifteen node test runners never execute in CI, and two structural guards are RED at HEAD in a runner nothing invokes. **Structure & hygiene (3/5)**: the live set is 273 documents / ~174k lines against ~177k lines of renderer TypeScript — a 1:1 docs-to-code ratio — of which only 13 documents sit one hop from an entry point, and 76% of that is two search-only ledgers.

| Lens | Score | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| DEVELOPMENT | **2/5** | One excellent developer doc, invisible from the entry point; four topics undocumented; two core procedures unfollowable at HEAD. |
| PLANNING | **3/5** | Best-in-repo orientation and row-card design; the row layer's facts are not maintained and every sizing figure is wrong. |
| SCOPING | **3/5** | High ceiling, low floor — complete scopes exist, but three trusted pointers are false and the "complete" rules sit in an untracked file. |
| WELL-BUILT | **2/5** | The debugging half is strong; the completeness half fails findability, truth and enforcement simultaneously. |
| STRUCTURE & HYGIENE | **3/5** | First-rate structural artifacts (AGENTS.md, charter §0.4, mechanically-enforced rules) inside a set that is code-sized and hub-dependent. |

**Mean 2.6/5.** The single highest-leverage fact: the auto-loaded rules file is untracked, so nothing in the repository can diff it, guard it, or ship it to a clone — the mechanism behind a large share of the drift below.
