### Latent note carried out of H-P9, not a reversal

`WebGPUPointCloudRenderer.ts:1789` sets `const revision = pointCount`, so the instance-buffer rebuild gate is keyed on point count rather than on a content revision. H-P9's dismissal is correct **because** no consumer mutates a `PointCloud`'s parsed content in place: each `TimeDynamicPointCloud` frame is a fresh instance, and tileset PNTS content is parsed once. The dismissal is therefore contingent on that invariant rather than on the gate being sound. This is the same shape as H-P11 and is worth one sentence in the new review's caching lens: two caches in the tree are keyed on a proxy for identity rather than on identity itself, and both are currently safe only because nothing mutates in place.
